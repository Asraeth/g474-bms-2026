For every approval record board revision, schematic revision, instrument/setup, firmware/GUI commits, profile/enable flags, measured result and approving engineer. Changing routing requires a new/updated profile and regenerated artifacts, not edits scattered through acquisition or GUI logic. Physical connector pins absent from supplied drawings remain explicitly unresolved.

Until these approvals exist, retain all physical confirmation flags at zero, energetic enable flags at zero, and Celsius calibration invalid. Single-slave communication/raw measurement testing can proceed within the staged BENCH scope.
